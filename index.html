Linkers of Contrast & Concession

LinkerPatternExample
although although + subject + verb Although cities are crowded, they offer more job opportunities.
even though even though + subject + verb Even though rural life is peaceful, it can sometimes feel isolating.
though though + subject + verb Though public transport is convenient, it can be overcrowded.
despite despite + noun / -ing Despite the high cost of living, many people move to cities.
despite the fact that despite the fact that + subject + verb Despite the fact that the city is crowded, she enjoys living there.
however sentence linker; often at the beginning of a new sentence Rural areas are less polluted. However, they may offer fewer job opportunities.
on the other hand introduces an opposing point City life can be stressful. On the other hand, it offers plenty of opportunities.
yet joins contrasting ideas The village is small, yet it has everything we need.
